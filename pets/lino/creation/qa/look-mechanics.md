Kernel's gaze mechanics

The seated body, front paws, hind paws, collar attachment and tail root remain anchored on the idle baseline. The physical head pitches up/down and yaws toward the viewer's right/left. Both green eyes retain their original iris, pupil and highlight construction; their gaze follows the head. The pink nose tip and white muzzle provide the primary direction landmarks. Ears follow head rotation naturally. The face remains readable, including at the up cardinal; never show a back-of-head view or rotate the whole cat. No replacement eyes or added props.

For up, lift the nose and muzzle and aim both eyes toward the top edge. For down, lower the nose/muzzle and eyelids toward the paws. For screen-right, shift the nose tip right of head center with a right-facing three-quarter muzzle and gaze. For screen-left, use the corresponding leftward head turn. Intermediate poses combine these motions evenly while preserving lower-body registration.
